# 0.1.35 — Spell scaling and combination inheritance

Status: implemented; integrated regression and native visual checks completed, final revision gate before merge. Current development scope; future expedition keyword designs remain separate. This record supersedes older combination penalties and the old exclusion of burst AoEs from Duration. V1.35 follows the existing release numbering as 0.1.35.

## Rules

- Six base spell slots and six passive slots remain. Combinations keep both ingredients and consume no extra slot.
- Ingredient **levels** contribute explicit base-relative bonuses to named components. Do not copy ingredient rank rewards (e.g. Bolt projectile count or Meteor Shower's meteor count) into a combination.
- Each combination has its own upgrade behavior. Apply Spell Power once to each damage/heal/protection component; do not multiply already-scaled ingredient results again.
- Power improves damage, healing, and protection. Size modifies actual collision/area geometry and corresponding visuals. Velocity modifies travel or turning where meaningful. Unsupported properties do not gain cosmetic-only effects.
- New Spell Duration passive: +10% base active lifetime per rank, additive. It is a ninth available passive family competing for six slots. It does not modify per-cast typing slowdown (Focus), warnings, arming delays, or tick spacing.
- A burst AoE has a short visible active lifetime. Impact is immediate after its usual warning; newly arriving enemies can take its damage until expiry. Each enemy is hit once per burst, not once per frame. Separate impacts have separate hit ledgers.
- Prism Ray is a broad penetrating laser with very slow tracking at 0.25 radians/second (about 14 degrees/second), 16 times slower than base Focus Ray. Origin follows the caster; target death triggers retargeting without snapping. Velocity does not accelerate Prism turning.
- No change to enemy pacing, disabled adaptive clear pressure, style thresholds, Atomic cost, typing slowdown base, or automatic attack progression in this release.

## Pickups

Non-boss deaths independently roll 1% for a health potion and 1% for a style rune. The rune adds 200 raw combo (normal cap) and 200 × the multiplier immediately before pickup to banked run score. It does not change cast accuracy, spell freshness/repetition, manual-cast counts, or decay grace. No repeat collection. No collection during pause, death, or finalized results. Health and style pickups are visually distinct.

- Frost Sigil final-rank second placement and broader spell rank caps need a separate decision. Its current arming-only progression stops offering upgrades at rank9 when the0.4-second floor is reached; no no-op level-up cards.
- Typed keyword composition, new spells, and campaign progression remain future work.
- Targeting/rank-curve/focus/offscreen changes mentioned in earlier batches are separate pending changes; Seeker target distribution and five-second style grace already shipped in0.1.31.
- Soul Bloom healing-carrier and leech proposals are superseded for this pass by death blooms.
- All coefficients need human playtesting. Monitor overlapping Lightning splashes, persistent infection healing, Prism line damage, Firewalk's two duration benefits, and orbit spacing.

## Validation gate

Failures remain open until reconciled. Record exact candidate and evidence before merge.

## Review and evidence

Independent source reviews covered component inheritance, duration semantics, projectile contact/pool reuse, finite healing, and pickup scoring; no remaining blockers were reported. The scaled infection retarget radius found during review was corrected. Native gameplay and Duration choice screens were inspected at1280×720 and640×480; button selection returns to play and retains the passive. A180-second accelerated bot smoke run (seed135) completed at level12 with51 casts and no runtime errors; this is a smoke test, not a20-minute balance verdict.

The release verification manifest and logs are saved under `builds/spell-scaling-0135/`. Deliberate negative controls disable Duration, accelerate Prism turning, and restore single-contact projectile behavior; each must fail its corresponding assertions.

Legacy assertions were reconciled with explicit current contracts: the orbit path scales with Size; shield recasts already extend remaining time; infection host limiting was already disabled; the Necronomicon already shows undiscovered recipes; combination penalties are now removed; version labels are0.1.35. No runtime changes were made to restore obsolete expectations.
