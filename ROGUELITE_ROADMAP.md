# Game roadmap

The current [release plan and expedition roadmap](docs/expedition-design/15-development-order.md) owns version numbers and sequencing. The complete [spell catalog](docs/expedition-design/03-spells.md) owns content; new combinations remain deferred. Start at the [design index](docs/expedition-design/README.md).

The former accumulated roadmap is retained as [historical planning](docs/expedition-design/archive/2026-10-14/ROGUELITE_ROADMAP.md), not a parallel priority list.

Spell-feel work starts from the [written sequence specification](docs/expedition-design/18-spell-system-rewrite.md): review the fantasy and connected cast, motion, contact, sound, camera and ending before building each particle-based prototype batch. Bring the existing roster to a consistent B in motion and combat before expanding the catalog. Spirit orbit/pass behavior remains a design choice.
