# Game roadmap

The current [release plan and expedition roadmap](docs/expedition-design/15-development-order.md) owns version numbers and sequencing. The complete [spell catalog](docs/expedition-design/03-spells.md) owns content; new combinations remain deferred. Start at the [design index](docs/expedition-design/README.md).

The former accumulated roadmap is retained as [historical planning](docs/expedition-design/archive/2026-10-14/ROGUELITE_ROADMAP.md), not a parallel priority list.

Spell-feel work starts from the [written sequence specification](docs/expedition-design/18-spell-system-rewrite.md): review the fantasy and connected cast, motion, contact, sound, camera and ending before building each particle-based prototype batch. Bring the existing roster to a consistent B− or better in motion and combat before expanding the catalog. Spirit orbit/pass behavior remains a design choice.

The [15 October spell audit](docs/expedition-design/18-spell-system-rewrite.md#current-spell-audit--15-october-2026) supplies provisional grades and fantasy-led prototype priorities. Complete the untested sound and play-response evidence before declaring any spell at the C−-minimum/B−-average target; preserve strong identities while repairing weak phases.

Both spirit-image prototypes were rejected. The current direction is an isolated animated-core and material-flow study, judged through hover, acceleration, turns, stopping and arrival before gameplay integration. The canonical spell-feel document records this as a roster-wide principle. Cross Blade and Atomic remain identity priorities after that focused study.
